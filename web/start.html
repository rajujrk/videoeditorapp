Get started

TechieVideoEditor is built from source on your Mac. These steps take you from a fresh checkout to your first export.

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon, on macOS 13 or later, with the Xcode Command Line Tools.
  • CMake 3.25 or later, Ninja, pkg-config and Qt 6.7 or later, from Homebrew.
  • vcpkg, which builds FFmpeg and the other libraries. Its first build takes a while.
brew install cmake ninja pkg-config qt
git clone https://github.com/microsoft/vcpkg ~/vcpkg && ~/vcpkg/bootstrap-vcpkg.sh

Build it

From the project folder:

export VCPKG_ROOT=~/vcpkg
cmake --preset macos-release -DCMAKE_PREFIX_PATH="$(brew --prefix qt)"
cmake --build --preset macos-release

Run it

build/macos-release/app/TechieVideoEditor.app/Contents/MacOS/TechieVideoEditor

Video files named after the command are imported and put on the main track; a .tve file is opened. Add --theme light for the light theme.

Your first edit

  1. On the Home screen, click a canvas shape, such as 16:9. Start a project
  2. Press ⌘I and pick a few videos, then drag them onto the timeline. Import media
  3. Drag clip edges to trim, and press S to split at the playhead. Move, trim and split
  4. Click a transition in the library's Transitions tab to put it on the nearest cut. Transitions
  5. Add a title from the Titles tab and type your words in the inspector. Title templates
  6. Press ⌘E, pick 1080p, and export. Export a video

Where your data goes

On this Mac

Captions, transcripts, speech clean-up, people, motion and picture analysis run on your Mac. Models download once, checked against their SHA-256, and nothing you edit is uploaded.

Your own key

Writing, translation, cloud voices, Copilot and Generate use keys you add in AI settings. Keys stay in your Mac's Keychain; what you send goes to that provider and is billed to your account.
